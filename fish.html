Fish

Let's learn more about the different types of fish!

Click on the pictures below for a bonus fun fact!

Salmon

Salmon turn colors when they grow up. They turn red!

Catfish

Catfish can walk on land! They use their fins to move across the ground.

Clownfish

Clownfish love to talk to each other underwater! They made tiny popping and clicking noises to do this.

Flounder

Flounder love to hide on the ocean floor, so when they grow up, one of their eyes move to the other side of their head. This way they can hide flat on the ocean floor without being seen!
